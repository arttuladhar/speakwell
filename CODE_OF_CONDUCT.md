# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We aim to make participation welcoming, respectful, and constructive. Contributors are expected to:

- Be considerate and patient
- Focus on the work and assume good faith
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences
- Give and receive feedback professionally
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive material

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository's contact or security-reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
